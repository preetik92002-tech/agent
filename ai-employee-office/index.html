      What is real in Phase 1?

      • Real: navigation, search, creating/assigning/moving tasks, activity log, saving in your browser.
      • Simulated: Anne's “routing” (keyword matching), all sample activity, employee statuses (derived from tasks).
      • Not built yet: AI models, voice, microphone, agent-to-agent messaging, approvals for real actions.

      Coming in Phase 2

      • Six independent agent profiles
      • Anne as real orchestrator (Python / FastAPI)
      • Local models via Ollama, or an optional hosted model
      • Shared task & message store, live employee chat
      • Speech recognition and distinct voices
      • Human approval for risky actions
